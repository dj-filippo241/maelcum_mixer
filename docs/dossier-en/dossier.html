1. What it is, design philosophy

Maelcum is a single, hand-built mixer for Filippo's own sound system (Horns & Bricks, 4 ways today and a fifth soon). It replaces the DJ mixer, a Soundcraft EPM6 and a Ligwa preamp, and feeds a dbx DriveRack VENU360 directly. The VENU360 handles crossover, delay and speaker protection (dec. 132). It is used outdoors.

It is a hybrid of three instruments, without forking into separate products (dec. 111):

  • DJ mixer: 4 stereo channels, isolator EQ, 100 mm faders, CUE, a global HPF/LPF filter in the style of the Xone:92/96.
  • Sound-system preamp: a 5-way performance EQ with one kill per physical way of the rig, and two saturators (GROWL, NASTRO).
  • Live dub desk: 4 FX buses to external pedals, with a 4×4 feedback matrix that includes self-feedback, plus mic and mono instrument inputs.

3.4 Polarity convention

  1. Every path from a source to the master XLR is non-inverting (XLR pin 2 in phase with the input).
  2. Every FX feedback loop (cross and self) is non-inverting.
  3. Every virtual-earth receiver counts −1 (DJ bus, HPF/LPF buses, FX buses, MASTER SUM, CUE sum). A new path is counted end to end across modules, not only inside one module.

A full audit on 2026-11-03 (docs/fase3/sistema-polarita.md) found four inverted paths (filter, MIC/MONO, reroute loop, CUE). Fixes: the filter overdrive cell outputs inverted; MIC/MONO MSUM drivers are inverting unity (20 k/20 k); the reroute injection driver is inverting unity (20 k/20 k 0.1 %). Note for netlists: the THAT 2181 / V2181 VCA plus I/V converter is non-inverting (datasheet Rev 03 p. 7); a voltage-mode VCA macro plus R, or an F source pushing current into the I/V node, has the opposite sign of the real chip.

6. FX-RET modules (×4) and feedback matrix

7. Global filter

Status v0.2 / v0.3-WIP. Reference character: Sherman Filterbank (resonance), Xone:96 (law). Six panel knobs: HPF FREQ/RES/DRIVE, LPF FREQ/RES/DRIVE.

7.1 Architecture LOCKED

  • Post-fader on bus sums (dec. 159): 4 mono cores. Equivalent to per-channel filtering while linear; differences accepted: DRIVE saturates the sum and follows the faders, one resonance whistle per section, no beating between cores. 15.5 Generator lessons

  • Nets touching TSSOP pins (0.65 mm pitch) need traces ≤ 0.3 mm, or Freerouting will not connect them.
  • Cluster gap around each op-amp (d.cluster_gap) 0.5 mm: at 0.2 mm Freerouting leaves nets open. Each R/C is bound to its op-amp pin and travels with the chip.
  • ±18 V supply traces at 0.4 mm: at 0.6 mm Freerouting left −18 V open on several boards.
  • Freerouting is not deterministic: a single PASS with a net at the limit is not proof.
  • Two adjacent pins of the same net on a header make Freerouting fail the whole net: alternate pins (+5 V, CTRL_GND, +5 V, CTRL_GND) or the link_pairs option (pre-routed straight link between twin through-hole pads).
  • Class rules come from a single source (Design._classrules): via sizes in the board and in .kicad_pro once differed and produced false clearance errors.
  • Decoupling 100 nF placed ~2 mm from its pin automatically; ground vias outward first, then under the chip body; fanout_more tries diagonal positions.
  • Design bug found by review of the generated schematic: FX-SEND assign mute left the ADG1434 IN at 1.21 V, between the 0.8 and 2.0 V thresholds. Fixed (100 k to ground, 1 k series): 4.55 V on, 0.45 V muted.

16.2 Simulation rules learned the hard way

  • Noise: never .meas INTEG on .noise; read density at 10–16 points. Only linear 4-node E sources propagate noise; a behavioural E does not.
  • SINE takes peak: 0 dBu = 1.095 Vp; +18 dBu = 8.70 Vp (6.16 V is rms; used as peak it is +15 dBu).
  • .tran with feedback loops needs .nodeset; op-amp macro in two variants (linear mirror for AC/noise, abs for transient). The single-pole OP1612 macro is always stable; stability work needs an inline second pole. OPA1612 current is 3.6 mA per channel, not 2.
  • Power supplies: transformer with open-circuit EMF and winding resistance, never ideal (rms winding current ~1.9× DC). Regulator loops: never uic, start from zero.
  • .step param ... list runs values in ascending order, not written order. A .param written twice with .step silences .four without an error.
  • OTA SVF at near-zero Iabc: .op does not converge; use .tran ... uic and measure peak-to-peak.

18.2 Engineering questions where experience would help most

  1. Nonlinear stages without a bench. GROWL and NASTRO band-split circuits exist only as models and LTspice. JFET spread (Idss, Vp bins), diode matching in the clippers, the noise penalty at full rotation (−71.1 dBu A with both) and the NASTRO 3/12 kHz runs that do not terminate. Is the parallel-blend topology robust to real parts? Would you bin JFETs or trim per unit?
  2. OTA filter core at project levels. The 1/30 divider trades noise and offset (×30) for headroom; the sum of channels hits a −1…+1.2 dBu ceiling at cutoff. Is there a better way to raise the ceiling without the noise penalty? Is the ~2× offset margin against the servo enough with real LM13700 lots?
  3. Long multidrop virtual-earth nets on a ~75 cm backplane. 12 contributors per FX bus, 10 k injection, Cf 10 pF, guarded stripline ≤ 0.2 pF. Crosstalk is −58 dB per pF at 20 kHz. Would you rather convert to current-mode or balanced internal buses, or receive locally and send low-Z?
  4. Grounding. Single star on PSU-IN, removable chassis bond, PE in the umbilical, external box. Outdoor rig with pedals and a turntable. Any trap you would expect with ~19 slots and a 2 m DC umbilical?
  5. Discrete MOSFET LDOs in the box. Stability across load steps and cable inductance, current limit at hot heatsink, soft start versus inrush of the mixer's bulk capacitors.
  6. VCA clone. V2181L with no electrical table; ~54 chips, symmetry trim on each, matching jig. Experience with lot-to-lot spread, and with law trims you could avoid.
  7. Analogue switches. ADG1434 π SPDT for kills, assigns and routing; zero-crossing switching; switches moved to virtual-earth nodes for headroom. Charge injection and off-isolation on a real layout.
  8. PCB density and generated layouts. 4 layers, inner AGND planes, both-side SMD, 380–410 footprints per module split over two boards with ribbon bridges. The audio-check thresholds (A1–A5) are guesses until a board is built. Review of a generated board (FX-SEND is the most mature) would calibrate them.
  9. Thermal. ~118–132 W inside the mixer, two thermostatic fans, 40 °C outdoor ambient, bootstrapped master stage at ~1 W per pass device.
  10. Isolator MFB cells with the OPA1654 under real capacitive loading; precision capacitor sourcing in E6 pairs.
